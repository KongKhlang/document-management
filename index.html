Frequently Asked Questions

What age group is LearnSphere designed for? ▼
LearnSphere is designed for students aged 7 to 17! Quests are categorized into Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced levels, allowing younger kids and teenagers to learn at their own pace.
Do we need to install any special software for coding or 3D art? ▼
No! All interactive coding sandboxes, Python environments, and 3D preview tools run directly inside your web browser. You only need an internet connection and a computer or tablet.
Can parents track their child's progress? ▼
Yes! Parents get access to a dedicated Parent Dashboard with weekly progress emails, time spent per subject, certificates earned, and skill mastery milestones.
Can I cancel or change my plan anytime? ▼
Absolutely. There are no contracts or hidden fees. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time with a single click in your settings.
